Open Ambient Sensor looks like a smoke detector on purpose. Inside are a Sensirion SEN66 (CO₂, PM, VOC, NOx, temperature, humidity), an mmWave radar that notices you even when you sit still, and an LED ring that breathes the colour of your air, all running ESPHome for Home Assistant.

It's a room sensor with a 35-stage build pipeline: the PCB is generated by Python and has to pass DRC, ERC and SPICE surge simulations before every order.

Every file is open, so you can build your own: https://github.com/hubertciebiada/open-ambient-sensor
