| Who is it for? | Home Assistant / ESPHome users who want per-room CO₂, particulates and real presence detection to drive automations (HVAC, ventilation), and who won't accept cheap sensors or a 3D-printed box on the wall. |

## Angle

A sensor that refuses both compromises: it measures like an instrument, hides in plain sight like a smoke detector, and is engineered with the paranoia of a production line. Everything is open, so you can build your own.

## Hook

A dark room. The real unit glows green through its perforations and breathes on the firmware's own 4-second envelope. On screen: "It looks like a smoke detector." Beat. "On purpose."

## Highlights

1. **What's inside:** the real board (KiCad 3D render, masked to its Ø120 mm D-shape) with the three parts that matter tagged in place: SEN66, LD2410C, ESP32-C6.
2. **Product in use:** the same glowing unit reacts to stale air. The colour comes from the firmware's AQI table, and Home Assistant switches the ventilation on.
3. **The brag:** the board draws itself from its real routing data while `python build.py` streams the 35 real stage names: "It's a room sensor. It has a 35-stage build pipeline."

## Punchline / outro

"Open Ambient Sensor. Every file is open. Build your own." The repo URL, with `v0.54 · built & validated · GPLv3` below it.

## Motion and critique (from "How to build motion design studio with Opus 5.5")

- **Springs, not easing curves.** Every entrance is a closed-form damped spring, which is still a pure function of t. UI (tags, toast) overshoots a hair, type and big objects do not. Exits accelerate out.
- **No dips to black.** Each cut carries one element across: the cover goes x-ray, the camera flies into the ring's light, the rim is traced over the cover, and the LEDs fly into the logomark.
- **Critique loop.** Twelve key frames were rendered and scored 1–10 after each pass.
  - Pass 1: most frames 8–9. Three fell short: the B→C zoom (6, a big half-transparent grey board), the start of the trace (6, an empty frame) and the outro (7, the "centred text + logo" cliché).
  - Pass 2: fixed with the bloom, an earlier and bolder trace, and the perforation halo. Every settled frame now scores 8+. The only frame below 8 is a 0.3 s mid-flight moment of the LEDs.

## Rules check

- Readable: every line holds at least 0.3 s per word after it has fully settled. Scrolling stage lines are texture.
- Transitions carry one element across (see above). Busy layouts never cross-dissolve: old text leaves before new text arrives.
- No invented claims. Card values (ppm, AQI) are illustrative readings of the product in use. The AQI numbers come from the firmware's own formula.
- Public-repo rules: English only, no personal data. The only identifier shown is the public repository URL, which is already printed on the silkscreen.
